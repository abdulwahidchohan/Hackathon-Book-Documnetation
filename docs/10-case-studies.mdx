## Overview

This chapter examines real-world humanoid robot deployments, research benchmarks, and lessons learned from successful and failed projects.

## Case Study 1: Boston Dynamics Atlas

### Overview
- **Developer**: Boston Dynamics
- **First Release**: 2013
- **Current Version**: Atlas (2023)
- **Height**: 1.5m, Weight: 89kg

### Key Capabilities
- Dynamic bipedal locomotion
- Parkour (running, jumping, backflips)
- Object manipulation
- Autonomous navigation

### Technical Innovations
- Hydraulic actuation for high power-to-weight ratio
- Model predictive control for balance
- Whole-body trajectory optimization
- Real-time perception and planning

### Lessons Learned
- Hydraulics provide power but require complex maintenance
- Robust control crucial for dynamic maneuvers
- Simulation-to-reality transfer needs careful tuning

## Case Study 2: Tesla Optimus

### Overview
- **Developer**: Tesla
- **Announcement**: 2021
- **Target**: General-purpose humanoid
- **Cost Goal**: &lt;$20,000

### Design Philosophy
- Leverage Tesla's AI infrastructure (FSD, Dojo)
- Vision-based control (no LiDAR)
- Mass production approach
- End-to-end learning

### Current Status (2024)
- Walking and basic manipulation demonstrated
- Factory deployment for simple tasks
- Ongoing development of dexterity

### Implications
- Potential for affordable humanoids
- Vision-only approach controversial
- Manufacturing scale could transform industry

## Research Benchmarks

### RoboCup Humanoid League

**Objective**: Robot soccer competition

**Categories**:
- Kid Size (40-90cm)
- Teen Size (80-140cm)
- Adult Size (130-180cm)

**Skills Tested**:
- Bipedal walking
- Ball detection and tracking
- Kicking
- Team coordination

**Recent Winners**:
- 2023: Team NimbRo (Germany)
- Key innovation: Deep learning for vision

### DARPA Robotics Challenge (2015)

**Tasks**:
1. Drive vehicle
2. Egress vehicle
3. Open door
4. Turn valve
5. Use power tool
6. Clear debris
7. Climb stairs
8. Final surprise task

**Winner**: Team KAIST (Korea) - DRC-HUBO

**Lessons**:
- Reliability > speed
- Teleoperation crucial for complex tasks
- Battery life critical constraint

### Humanoid Robot Benchmarks

**Locomotion**:
- Walking speed: 1.5-2.5 m/s
- Stair climbing: 15-20 cm step height
- Rough terrain: Uneven surfaces, slopes

**Manipulation**:
- Grasp success rate: &gt;90%
- Object weight: 5-10 kg
- Task completion time

**Autonomy**:
- Navigation accuracy: &lt;10 cm error
- Obstacle avoidance: 100% success
- Battery life: 2-4 hours

## Failed Projects and Lessons

### ASIMO (Retired 2018)

**Achievements**:
- Pioneer in bipedal walking
- Smooth, human-like gait
- Stair climbing, running

**Why Retired**:
- Limited practical applications
- High cost, low ROI
- Shift to AI-driven approaches

**Lessons**:
- Technical excellence ≠ commercial success
- Need clear use cases
- Market timing matters

### Pepper (Discontinued 2021)

**Concept**: Social companion robot

**Issues**:
- Limited functionality
- High expectations vs. reality
- Maintenance costs
- Privacy concerns

**Lessons**:
- Social robotics is hard
- Novelty wears off
- Need genuine value proposition

## Summary

The field is transitioning from research demonstrations to commercial products.
