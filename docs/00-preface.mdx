## Overview

This book aims to provide a comprehensive, research-backed foundation for understanding and developing AI humanoid robotics. It integrates core concepts from AI (perception, control, planning, cognition) with practical aspects of robotics hardware, software development, and ethical considerations. The content is designed for robotics engineers, AI/ML practitioners, and graduate students in related fields.

## Scope

**In Scope:**
- Practical, research-backed foundations of humanoid robotics integrating AI.
- AI-native software development practices for robotics: spec-driven workflows, CI/CD, testing, simulation.
- Reproducible examples, minimal working demos, and deployment guidance.
- Coverage of key areas: AI Fundamentals, Humanoid Hardware & Sensors, Perception & State Estimation, Motion Planning & Control, Language/Multimodal/Cognitive Models, AI-Native Software Development, Simulation & Tooling, Safety/Ethics & Governance, Case Studies & Benchmarks, Future Directions & Research Agenda.

**Out of Scope:**
- Full hardware manufacturing guides or PCB designs.
- Vendor comparisons or procurement catalogs.
- Proprietary datasets or closed-source firmware.
- Detailed ethical-legal frameworks beyond robotics safety essentials.
- Extensive mathematical proofs; the focus is on applied, well-cited summaries with references.

## Book Structure

This book is organized into the following chapters:

- **00: Preface & Scope**: Current chapter.
- **01: Introduction to AI Humanoid Robotics**: Foundational concepts and challenges.
- **02: AI Fundamentals for Robotics**: Core AI/ML/DL for edge deployment.
- **03: Humanoid Hardware & Sensors**: Components and sensing modalities.
- **04: Perception & State Estimation**: Environmental understanding and sensor fusion.
- **05: Motion Planning & Control**: Algorithms for robot movement.
- **06: Language, Multimodal & Cognitive Models**: LLMs/VLMs for higher-level functions.
- **07: AI-Native Software Development**: Modern practices for robotics software.
- **08: Simulation & Tooling**: Platforms for development and data generation.
- **09: Safety, Ethics & Governance**: Responsible robotics development.
- **10: Case Studies & Benchmarks**: Real-world applications and evaluations.
- **11: Future Directions & Research Agenda**: Emerging trends and open questions.
- **References**: Comprehensive list of all cited sources.
