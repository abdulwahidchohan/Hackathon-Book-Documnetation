## Overview

This book aims to provide a comprehensive, research-backed foundation for understanding and developing AI humanoid robotics. It integrates core concepts from AI (perception, control, planning, cognition) with practical aspects of robotics hardware, software development, and ethical considerations. The content is designed for robotics engineers, AI/ML practitioners, and graduate students in related fields.

## Scope

**In Scope:**
- Practical, research-backed foundations of humanoid robotics integrating AI.
- AI-native software development practices for robotics: spec-driven workflows, CI/CD, testing, simulation.
- Reproducible examples, minimal working demos, and deployment guidance.
- Coverage of key areas: AI Fundamentals, Humanoid Hardware & Sensors, Perception & State Estimation, Motion Planning & Control, Language/Multimodal/Cognitive Models, AI-Native Software Development, Simulation & Tooling, Safety/Ethics & Governance, Case Studies & Benchmarks, Future Directions & Research Agenda.

**Out of Scope:**
- Full hardware manufacturing guides or PCB designs.
- Vendor comparisons or procurement catalogs.
- Proprietary datasets or closed-source firmware.
- Detailed ethical-legal frameworks beyond robotics safety essentials.
- Extensive mathematical proofs; the focus is on applied, well-cited summaries with references.

## Terminology Glossary

- **Physical AI**: AI systems that function in the real world, comprehending physical laws and interacting with the environment through embodied intelligence.
- **Humanoid Robotics**: The design and study of robots with a human-like form, aimed at navigating human-centered environments.
- **ROS 2 (Robot Operating System 2)**: A modular middleware providing communication infrastructure for robotic systems, built on the DDS (Data Distribution Service) standard.
- **VLA Models (Vision-Language-Action)**: AI models that integrate perception, language understanding, and motor control to execute task-based commands.
- **SLAM (Simultaneous Localization and Mapping)**: The process of creating a map of an unknown environment while simultaneously keeping track of the robot's location.
- **NVIDIA Isaac**: A comprehensive platform for robotics development, including simulation (Isaac Sim) and accelerated perception (Isaac ROS).
- **Embodied Intelligence**: The principle that intelligence emerges from the tight coupling of an agent's body, its control system (brain), and its physical environment.
- **MPC (Model Predictive Control)**: A method of process control that relies on dynamic models of the system to optimize future behavior.
- **Digital Twin**: A virtual representation of a physical object or system, used in simulation to test behaviors before real-world deployment.
